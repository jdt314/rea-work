How we handled
your data?
CapWords does not store any user-captured photos. When a user takes a photo, the image is temporarily transmitted to a third-party AI model for one-time recognition. It is immediately discarded after processing — not saved locally on the device, and not uploaded to any CapWords server (as CapWords does not operate any servers).
How does
CapWords work?
CapWords currently uses Apple’s native system-level capabilities, including:
AVSpeechSynthesizer:
for high-quality speech playback (with Neural Voice support)
VisionKit and AVFoundation: for image capture and background removal
CloudKit: for syncing users’ review progress and image-based content between iPhone and iPad
Speech and SFSpeechRecognizer: for voice recognition functionality

We’ve got answers
Find answers to the most common questions about
using our app on your mobile device.
What is CapWords?
CapWords is a language learning app that helps you learn new words by snapping photos of real-world objects and turning them into vocabulary stickers.
How does CapWords work?
Just take a photo of any object. CapWords recognizes it and gives you the word, pronunciation, and sticker in your target language.
Do I need an account to use CapWords?
No, CapWords works without registration. You can start learning right away.
Is CapWords safe? How does it handle my photos?
CapWords processes images locally on your device and does not upload or store your photos on any server.
Can kids use CapWords?
Yes! CapWords is simple, visual, and safe, perfect for learners of all ages.
