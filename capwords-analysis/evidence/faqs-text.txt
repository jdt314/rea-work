General
What is CapWords?
CapWords is a language learning app that helps you learn new words by snapping photos of real-world objects and turning them into vocabulary stickers.
How does CapWords work?
Just take a photo of any object. CapWords recognizes it and gives you the word, pronunciation, and sticker in your target language.
Do I need an account to use CapWords?
No, CapWords works without registration. You can start learning right away.
Is CapWords safe? How does it handle my photos?
CapWords processes images locally on your device and does not upload or store your photos on any server.
Can kids use CapWords?
Yes! CapWords is simple, visual, and safe, perfect for learners of all ages.
CapWords Pro & Subscription
CapWords Pro &
Subscription
What is included in CapWords Pro?
CapWords Pro unlocks unlimited word captures, access to full review features, and early access to new features.
How do I start my free trial?
New users can try CapWords Pro for free after their initial free captures.
What happens when my free trial ends?
You can continue learning by subscribing to CapWords Pro or keep using limited free features.
What subscription plans are available?
CapWords offers monthly and yearly subscription options.
How can I cancel my subscription?
You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings.
What happens if my subscription expires?
You will still have access to your saved stickers but new captures will be limited.
App Features
Which languages does CapWords support?
CapWords supports 10+ languages including English, Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Thai, and more.
Can I switch my learning language and native language at any time?
Yes! You can change your learning language and update your native language in the settings (the native language can be changed only once per week).
Can I save my word stickers?
Yes, you can save stickers to build your personal word library.
Does CapWords work offline?
Word recognition requires internet access, but saved stickers can be reviewed offline.
How accurate is the object recognition?
CapWords uses advanced AI models for high accuracy. Results may vary with lighting or complex items.
How do I review the words I’ve learned?
Use the Review feature to revisit and practice previously saved stickers.
Devices & Availability
Is CapWords available on Android?
Currently CapWords is only available for iOS.
Can I use CapWords on iPad or Mac?
Yes, CapWords works on iPhone and iPad. Mac version is not yet available.
Can I sync CapWords across devices?
CapWords data stays on your device. Syncing across devices is not currently supported.
Privacy & Data
Are my photos or data uploaded to servers?
No, CapWords processes everything locally and never uploads your images.
How is my personal data handled?
We do not collect any personal data. Your data stays with you.
How can I report a wrong translation or recognition?
You can tap and manually edit any sticker to correct the word.
Tips & Troubleshooting
What should I do if the app can't recognize an object?
Try to take a clearer photo with good lighting and fewer background distractions.
How can I manually edit or correct a word?
Tap the sticker and select "Edit" to update the word.
How do I get support or share feedback?
Contact us via the in-app feedback form or email
support@capwords.app
